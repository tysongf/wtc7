What this view does and does not show
  • Shapes only. The run outputs keep node positions at the snapshots and nothing else: no stress, plastic strain or temperature field. The mesh is rebuilt from each run's configuration and matches the saved first snapshot.
  • Snapshots are taken at each 50 °C of beam temperature and at each column 79 bolt failure, so the 700 °C hold has none. Where a run walks off during the hold, only the trace shows it.
  • Displacements are rounded to {{ resText }} in. for this page.
  • Shells are drawn at their mid-surface; plate thickness is not drawn. Column faces, seats and side plates are the rigid contact planes of the model, not meshed columns.
  • Axes: x east, y north (column 79 to column 44 along the girder), z up; inches.
