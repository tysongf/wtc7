# Phase 3 solver verification: 3D frame sudden column removal vs. CalculiX 2.20

Generated by `phase3/verify_benchmark.py` (`docs/phase3_preregistration.md` Section 8).

## Benchmark setup

- **Structure:** 2-bay (`2 x 240 in.` along `X`) x 2-bay (`2 x 240 in.` along `Y`) x 3-storey (`3 x 144 in.` along `Z`) 3D steel moment frame (`E = 29,000 ksi`, `nu = 0.30`).
- **Sections:** `10 x 10 in.` columns (`9` column lines), `6 x 12 in.` biaxial floor girders (`36` girders across 3 floors).
- **Gravity load & mass:** Full member self-weight plus distributed tributary slab mass carried in the floor girders (`10 x rho_steel`).
- **Trigger:** Settle static equilibrium under full gravity, then suddenly remove the ground-storey interior center column at `(X = 240, Y = 240, Z = 0..144 in.)` over `t_r = 0.004 s` with zero damping during the dynamic response (`0.30 s`).
- **Pre-registered acceptance criterion:** Peak vertical dynamic deflection at the removed column line within **10 %** of CalculiX 2.20 (`*DYNAMIC`).
